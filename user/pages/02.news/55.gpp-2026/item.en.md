Hematology Center LLP has successfully completed the accreditation procedure and confirmed its compliance with the requirements of Good Pharmacy Practice (GPP) in the field of medicinal product circulation.

Based on the results of an inspection assessment conducted by the Committee for Medical and Pharmaceutical Control of the Ministry of Healthcare of the Republic of Kazakhstan, the organization has been issued a certificate of compliance with the established standards.

Good Pharmacy Practice (GPP) is a system of requirements for the storage, recording, dispensing, and quality control of medicines, aimed at ensuring their safety, efficacy, and proper use.

Confirmation of compliance with these standards is of fundamental importance, as it guarantees adherence to all stages of pharmaceutical supply for patients, minimizes risks associated with drug circulation, and improves the quality of medical care.

The receipt of this certificate reflects the Center's systematic work in implementing international approaches to the quality and safety of pharmaceutical activities.
