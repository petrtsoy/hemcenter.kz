The Republican Public Association "Kazakhstan Society of Hematologists", in cooperation with Tatyana Fyodorovna Baranik, the Chief Independent Hematologist of Astana, and "Hematology Center" LLP, continues a series of educational seminars for primary health care (PHC) physicians in Astana.

---

The purpose of these events is to improve the early diagnosis of hematological and onco-hematological diseases, enhance skills in interpreting laboratory tests, and optimize patient routing at the PHC level.

We would like to express our gratitude to the Astana Public Health Department and the heads of polyclinics for their assistance and support in organizing these events.
