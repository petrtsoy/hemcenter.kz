Hematology Center and the Blood Center invite everyone to join the Donor Day! 🩸

#### 📍 17 S.Seifullin Ave, Karaganda, Conference Hall
#### 📅 March 18, 2026 | 🕘 9:00 AM — 2:00 PM

---

### Who can become a donor:
* **Age:** 18 years and older
* **Weight:** at least 50 kg
* **Health:** Feeling well
* **Documents:** ID Card / Passport

---

### ⚠️ VERY IMPORTANT!

#### **48 hours before – Avoid:**
* ❌ Alcohol

#### **24 hours before – Avoid:**
* ❌ Fatty, fried, smoked foods
* ❌ Dairy products, eggs
* ❌ Nuts, seeds, bananas

---

### ☕ Morning of the donation — Have a light breakfast!

**Recommended foods:**
* ✅ Porridge (water-based)
* ✅ Vegetables, fruits
* ✅ Pasta (no oil/butter)
* ✅ Crackers, bread
* ✅ Sweet tea, water

---

> **By giving blood, you save lives!**
> **Blood donation is safe and essential!**
